Our 7-rule check

  1. Form: won 3 of the last 5, never lost twice in a row
  2. Home record: won 3 of the last 4 home games
  3. Away record: won 3 of the last 4 away games, the opponent lost 2 of their last 4 at home, and the away team is top 4 with the home team bottom 6 (internationals: away top 10 in the FIFA ranking, home ranked 50 or lower)
  4. Head to head: won 2 of the last 3 meetings
  5. Goals: scored 2 or more in 3 of the last 5
  6. Odds: single odd between 1.20 and 1.50
  7. Defence: scored 8 or more in the last 5 (scoring in 4 of them), conceded 5 or fewer with 2 or more clean sheets
