Our 6-rule check

  1. Form: won 3 of the last 5, never lost twice in a row
  2. Home or away record: the team we back won 3 of its last 4 games at that venue
  3. Head to head: won 2 of the last 3 meetings
  4. Goals: scored 2 or more in 3 of the last 5
  5. Odds: single odd between 1.20 and 1.50
  6. Total goals: scored 8 or more in the last 5
  • 6 of 6 rules: Ultra Safe
  • 5 of 6 rules: Safe
  • 4 of 6 rules: Value (it missed 2 rules)
  • Fewer than 4: left out

We back the stronger team: the away team in the top 4 against a home team in the bottom 6 (internationals: away team in the FIFA top 10, home team ranked 50 or lower).
